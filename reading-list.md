# Reading list

Roughly in suggested order. ★ = start here.

## Foundations
- ★ Sutton & Barto, *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction* (free online)
- OpenAI, *Spinning Up in Deep RL*
- ★ Doya (1999), *What are the computations of the cerebellum, the basal ganglia and the cerebral cortex?*
- Doya (2002), *Metalearning and neuromodulation*
- McClelland, McNaughton & O'Reilly (1995), *Complementary Learning Systems*

## Architecture
- ★ LeCun (2022), *A Path Towards Autonomous Machine Intelligence*
- ★ Sumers et al. (2023), *Cognitive Architectures for Language Agents* (CoALA)
- Goyal, Bengio et al. (2021), *Coordination Among Neural Modules Through a Shared Global Workspace*
- Ha & Schmidhuber (2018), *World Models*; Hafner et al., the Dreamer papers
- Park et al. (2023), *Generative Agents*; Wang et al. (2023), *Voyager*
- Janner et al. (2022), *Diffuser*; Chi et al. (2023), *Diffusion Policy*

## Emotion
- Damasio, *Descartes' Error*
- Panksepp, *Affective Neuroscience*
- Barrett, *How Emotions Are Made* (the opposing view)
- Moerland et al. (2018), *Emotion in Reinforcement Learning Agents and Robots*
- Bach, *Principles of Synthetic Intelligence* (MicroPsi)
- Picard, *Affective Computing*

## Development: learning from nothing
- ★ Cangelosi & Schlesinger, *Developmental Robotics: From Babies to Robots* (MIT Press)
- Vong, Wang, Orhan & Lake (2024), *Grounded language acquisition through the eyes and ears of a single child* (Science): word learning from a child's head-camera footage
- The BabyLM Challenge: training language models on roughly a child's amount of words
- Spelke, *What Babies Know* (core knowledge)
- The iCub robot project (a child-sized robot for developmental research)

## Sleep and memory
- ★ Walker, *Why We Sleep* (readable; some claims have been criticized as overstated)
- Tononi & Cirelli, *Sleep and the Price of Plasticity* (synaptic homeostasis)
- Hinton et al. (1995), *The wake-sleep algorithm*
- Tadros et al. (2022), *Sleep-like unsupervised replay reduces catastrophic forgetting*
- Schaul et al. (2015), *Prioritized Experience Replay*

## Personality and identity over time
- ★ Bouton, research on extinction, renewal and spontaneous recovery (e.g. *Context and behavioral processes in extinction*, 2004)
- Ryan, Roy, Pignatelli, Arons & Tonegawa (2015), *Engram cells retain memory under retrograde amnesia* (Science)
- Fleeson (2001), personality as density distributions of states (whole trait theory)
- McAdams, *The Stories We Live By* (narrative identity)
- Locke, *Essay Concerning Human Understanding*, Book II ch. 27 (personal identity)
- Parfit, *Reasons and Persons*, Part 3
- Hu et al. (2021), *LoRA*; Ilharco et al. (2023), *Editing Models with Task Arithmetic*
- Masse, Grant & Freedman (2018), *Alleviating catastrophic forgetting using context-dependent gating and synaptic stabilization*

## Small, dynamic networks
- ★ Benna & Fusi (2016), *Computational principles of synaptic memory consolidation* (Nature Neuroscience)
- Fusi, Drew & Abbott (2005), *Cascade models of synaptically stored memories*
- ★ Aso & Rubin (2016), *Dopaminergic neurons write and update memories with cell-type-specific rules* (eLife): the fly mushroom body
- FlyWire Consortium (2024), the whole fruit-fly brain connectome (Nature)
- Mocanu et al. (2018), *SET*; Evci et al. (2020), *RigL* (prune and regrow)
- Mallya & Lazebnik (2018), *PackNet*; Wortsman et al. (2020), *Supermasks in Superposition*

## The neuron
- ★ Cichon & Gan (2015), *Branch-specific dendritic Ca²⁺ spikes cause persistent synaptic plasticity* (Nature): different memories on different dendrite branches
- Bittner et al. (2017), *Behavioral time scale synaptic plasticity underlies CA1 place fields* (Science): one-shot learning
- Larkum (2013), *A cellular mechanism for cortical associations* (Trends in Neurosciences): apical dendrites and context
- Iyer et al. (2022), *Avoiding Catastrophe: Active Dendrites Enable Multi-Task Learning*

## Neuroscience
- Kandel et al., *Principles of Neural Science* (the standard textbook; use it as a reference)
- Eagleman, *Livewired* (plasticity, readable)

## Mind and soul
- Gazzaniga, *Who's in Charge?* (split brain, the interpreter)
- Minsky, *The Society of Mind* and *The Emotion Machine*
- Hofstadter, *I Am a Strange Loop*
- Butlin, Long et al. (2023), *Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence*
- Sutton, *The Bitter Lesson* (the counterargument)

## Making it small
- Bartol et al. (2015), *Nanoconnectomic upper bound on the variability of synaptic plasticity* (eLife): about 4.7 bits per real synapse
- Knight & Nowotny (2021), *Larger GPU-accelerated brain simulations with procedural connectivity* (Nature Computational Science)
- Triton (OpenAI): writing GPU kernels in Python-like syntax
