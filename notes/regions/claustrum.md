# Claustrum: neuron types and proportions (brief)

Confidence: ● well established · ◐ supported, numbers vary · ○ uncertain.

A thin sheet of grey matter between the insula and the striatum (external and extreme capsules). It has the densest
connectivity per volume of any forebrain structure: reciprocal links with almost all of cortex. Developmentally it is
close to deep cortical layers (L6, especially L6 IT Car3 cells share its molecular profile).

## 2. Signature behaviors to model

| Type | Behavior | Test idea |
|---|---|---|
| PN | regular spiking with subtype differences: PN1 minimal frequency adaptation, no ADP; PN3 strong initial doublet then steady; PN5 delayed first spike | step current: PN3 doublet, PN5 delay |
| PN (network) | **drives cortical inhibition**: output synapses preferentially onto cortical interneurons (PV and neurogliaform), giving feedforward inhibition of cortical pyramids (Jackson 2018) | stimulating claustrum lowers PFC pyramidal firing |
| PN (state) | synchronous claustral firing **triggers cortical slow waves / down-states** across many areas (Narikiyo 2020) | claustrum burst → simultaneous down-state in all connected areas |
| PV-IN | fastest, regular, highest rate, shortest spike | as cortical PV |
| VIP-IN | most excitable (lowest threshold, highest input resistance) | as cortical VIP |
| SST-IN | low adaptation, intermediate properties | as cortical SST |

## 3. Main connections

- **In:** nearly all of cortex, strongest from **prefrontal/anterior cingulate**, also sensory and motor (L6 IT Car3 and L5);
  midline/intralaminar thalamus; basolateral amygdala; neuromodulators (cholinergic basal forebrain, LC, raphe, VTA).
- **Out:** back to nearly all of cortex, both hemispheres, strongest to **prefrontal, cingulate, retrosplenial**
  (the "prefrontal module"), mostly onto interneurons; some subcortical targets (PN1). Single mouse claustral neurons can
  have very widespread cortical axons.
- Topography: claustral zones map onto cortical modules (e.g. visual, somatomotor), but with large overlap.

## 4. Human vs rodent

- In primates the claustrum is a distinct sheet separated from insula by the extreme capsule; in rodents it is less clearly bounded
  (merges with the endopiriform nucleus and insular L6), which complicates counts.
- No human proportions available.

## 5. Proposed role (for our model)

Coordinator of cortical state: it receives the prefrontal "what matters now" signal and broadcasts synchronized
inhibition to many areas (attention under distraction; slow-wave sleep coordination). Crick & Koch (2005) proposed a
role in binding conscious experience; this remains speculative.
