# LittleBrain store description

**Short description**

Watch 6,272 simulated neurons at work as a character learns which button to press and when to hide.

**Store description**

A picture flashes. Four buttons are available. Which one will the brain choose? LittleBrain lets you watch the choice unfold. The live map shows activity in the simulated regions, and the side panel shows the picture the character sees, the odds of each action, and the reward it receives.

In the signal game, each picture has a rewarding button. In the danger game, a cue can mean food or trouble, and hiding can spare the character a penalty. Pause on an unexpected choice, hover over a neuron to inspect its type, or follow the dopamine trace to see how the outcome compared with the brain’s expectation.

The Linux release includes the 6,272-neuron model. Empty outlines on the map mark regions outside this version of the simulation.
